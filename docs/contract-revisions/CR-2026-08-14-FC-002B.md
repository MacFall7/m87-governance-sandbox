# Contract Revision CR-2026-08-14-FC-002B

**Status:** Proposed — effective only when this revision is reviewed and merged.

## Decision requested

Expand the frozen failure matrix from 22 to 23 cases by adding `FC_002b`, and expand the executable suite from 23 to 24 tests.

## Gap

`FC_002` proves that a risk increase after manifest signing invalidates the manifest. It does not prove the paired semantic boundary: a risk decrease must leave the signed manifest valid. Reverting the reducer comparison from ordinal `>` to inequality `!==` would therefore pass the existing suite while restoring the demonstrated defect.

## Proposed contract

- **ID:** `FC_002b`
- **Injection:** Risk class de-escalation after manifest signing.
- **Expected:** Preserve the manifest, retain `IN_PROGRESS`, update the risk class, and emit no `risk_escalation_requires_resign` escalation.
- **Failure if:** The manifest is invalidated or the ticket becomes blocked solely because risk decreased.

## Governance consequence

The matrix and injection registry remain frozen. This revision does not weaken that boundary: the review and merge are the explicit authority event that permits their coordinated update. Future matrix changes still require their own visible contract revision.

## Verification

Approval requires all 24 tests to pass and TypeScript to report zero errors. The new test must fail when the reducer's ordinal comparison is replaced with inequality.
